## Things that need a second person

| Action | Who starts it | Who approves it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discount over the cashier's limit, price change at the till | Cashier | Manager, with their PIN at the till |
| Refund (if the shop's settings say so) | Cashier | Manager, with their PIN |
| Cash drop, payout from the drawer | Cashier | Manager, as witness, with their PIN |
| Cash-up | Cashier counts | Manager signs off |
| New price | Admin | Owner |
| Promotion | Owner, admin or manager | Owner |
| Purchase order | Owner, admin or accountant | Manager, owner or admin (not the same person) |
| Stock adjustment, transfer, stock count | Storekeeper | Manager (not the same person) |
| Return to supplier | Purchasing staff | Manager |
| Expense (office) | Owner, admin, manager or accountant | Manager, owner or admin (not the same person) |

## Daily checklist

**Opening:** sign in at the till and check the float. Look at the dashboard and the notifications bell.

**During the day:** drop excess cash to the safe. Record every payout at the till.

**Closing:** every cashier ends their shift and counts the drawer. The manager signs off the cash-ups, checks *M-PESA reconciliation → Not on a sale*, and checks the *eTIMS monitor* for anything that needs attention.

**Weekly:** check low stock and raise purchase orders; check *Past due* in Customer accounts; count one or two shelves.

**Monthly:** a full stock count; send the month-end reports to the accountant (chapter 11); check licences.

## Glossary

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Adjustment** | A change to stock outside a sale, delivery or transfer, such as a breakage. Needs a manager's approval. |
| **Aging** | What is owed, split by how long it has been owed (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days). |
| **Audit log** | The record of who did what, and when. Nobody can change it. |
| **Back office** | The part of Tessera used on an office computer: everything except the till. |
| **Blind count** | A stock count or cash count where the expected figure is hidden until the count is submitted. |
| **Cash drop** | Moving cash from the till drawer to the safe during a shift. |
| **Cash-up** | Counting the drawer at the end of a shift and comparing it with what was expected. |
| **Credit note** | The opposite of an invoice: it records money given back, for example for a return. |
| **eTIMS** | KRA's Electronic Tax Invoice Management System. Tessera sends it every sale and return. |
| **Float** | The cash in the drawer at the start of a shift, for giving change. |
| **GRN** | Goods-received note: the record of a delivery received against a purchase order. |
| **KRA PIN** | A person's or business's tax number, printed on tax invoices. |
| **Ledger (stock ledger)** | The full history of every stock movement. Stock figures come from it. |
| **M-PESA payment request** | A prompt sent to the customer's phone to approve a payment with their M-PESA PIN (STK Push). |
| **Park (hold) a sale** | Put a sale aside to serve someone else, and recall it later. |
| **Payout** | Cash paid out of the till drawer for an expense. |
| **Purchase order (PO)** | What you are ordering from a supplier. Approved by a second person. |
| **Quarantine** | A place for damaged items and opened returns. They cannot be sold. |
| **Reconciliation** | Checking that two records agree, for example M-PESA payments and sales. |
| **Reorder level** | The stock level at which an item counts as low and should be ordered. |
| **Role** | What a person is allowed to see and do: Owner, Admin, Branch Manager, Cashier, Storekeeper, Accountant. |
| **Shift** | One cashier on one till, from signing in to counting the cash. |
| **SKU** | Stock-keeping unit: the short code for one size of one product, such as *JWB-750*. |
| **Tot** | A measure of spirit poured from an open bottle and sold by the glass. |
| **Transfer** | Moving stock between branches, or from the store to the shop floor. |
| **Variance** | The difference between what was counted and what was expected. |
| **VAT** | Value Added Tax (16% on most drinks), included in shelf prices. |
| **Void** | Removing items, or a whole sale, before it is paid. |
| **Walk-in customer** | A customer who is not registered. Most sales are to walk-in customers. |
| **Wholesale customer** | A registered business that pays wholesale prices. |
