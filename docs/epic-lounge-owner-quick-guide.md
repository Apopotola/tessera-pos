# Owner's quick guide: Epic Lounge Social House

For **Brian**, the owner. Keep it next to the computer. What to check every day, every week and every month, how to do the common jobs, and what to do when something goes wrong.

- **Back office:** https://pos.tesseratech.co.ke (sign in with your email and password)
- **Till:** https://pos.tesseratech.co.ke/till (staff sign in with their name and PIN)
- **Help:** Tessera support, `support@tesseratech.co.ke`

The full instructions for every screen are in the **Epic Lounge User Guide**.

## Every day (10 minutes, after closing)

| ☐ | Check | Where | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ☐ | **Today's sales** | Dashboard | Sales, profit and the number of sales compared with a normal day. |
| ☐ | **Cash-ups signed off** | *Sales & Shifts → Shifts & cash-ups* | Every shift closed and signed off by a manager. Read the reason for any shortage. |
| ☐ | **Cash shortages** | Dashboard, notifications bell | Anything short by more than KES 100 raises an alert. The same cashier short again and again is a warning sign. |
| ☐ | **M-PESA codes matched** | *Payments → M-PESA reconciliation* | Every code a cashier typed should match a payment on your M-PESA statement. Chase any code with no payment the same day. |
| ☐ | **Voids and discounts** | *Reports → Voids, discounts & price overrides* | Who gave what away, and which manager approved it. |
| ☐ | **Approvals waiting** | Dashboard | Price changes, promotions, purchase orders and adjustments waiting for you. |
| ☐ | **Notifications** | The bell at the top | Low stock, licence reminders, unconfirmed M-PESA payments. |

> **Tip:** The money for the day is right when: cash-ups are signed off, cash in the safe matches the cash drops and counts, and every M-PESA code is matched.

## How do I…

| I want to… | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Change a price** | *Catalogue → Products* → the drink → **⋯** on the size → *Change price*. Choose retail, wholesale or tot, the new price, when it starts and why. |
| **Run a happy hour** | *Catalogue → Promotions → New promotion*: % off, the drinks or categories, the days and the hours. Check the yellow summary, save, then **Approve**. |
| **Add a new drink** | *Catalogue → Products → New product*. Scan the barcode from the bottle; choose the tax class carefully. Add a photo. |
| **Add a staff member** | *Administration → Users & roles → Add user*: name, role, phone or email. Then **⋯ → Set till PIN**. Cashiers need no password. |
| **Someone leaves** | *Users & roles* → the person → **Edit** → untick **Account active**. Do it the same day. |
| **Reset a forgotten password** | *Users & roles* → the person → **⋯ → Reset password** → type a temporary password and give it to them privately. They choose their own when they sign in. If you forget your own password, Tessera support resets it. |
| **Reset a forgotten PIN** | *Users & roles* → the person → **⋯ → Change till PIN**. |
| **Approve a price or promotion** | *Catalogue → Price changes* or *Promotions* → **Approve** or **Reject**. |
| **Order stock** | *Purchasing → Purchase orders → New*. You can approve it yourself, or approve one a manager raised. |
| **Receive a delivery** | *Inventory → Receive stock* → choose the purchase order → count what came, enter good and damaged → **Post to stock**. |
| **Record a breakage** | *Inventory → Breakages & adjustments → New adjustment* → a manager (or you) approves. |
| **Give a customer a tab (credit)** | *Customers* → register the customer → set a credit limit. Only if customer credit is switched on. |
| **Find a sale** | *Sales & Shifts → Sales*: search by receipt number, date, cashier or customer. |
| **See who did something** | *Administration → Audit log*: filter by person, date or action. |
| **Change what is on the receipt** | *Administration → Settings → Receipts*: header and footer lines. |

## Who does what at the lounge

| Role | Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You (Owner)** | Everything in the shop; the only one who approves prices and promotions. | Tessera-only settings (KRA PIN, eTIMS). Ask Tessera support. |
| **Branch Manager** | Approve discounts, voids, refunds and cash drops at the till; sign off cash-ups; receive stock; approve breakages. | Change prices. Approve their own cash-up or breakage. |
| **Cashier** | Sell, take payment, hold table tabs, ask for returns, count their drawer. | Anything in the back office. |
| **Storekeeper** | Receive deliveries, record breakages, move stock, count. | Approve their own adjustments. Sell. |
| **Accountant** | Purchase orders, supplier bills and payments, expenses, all reports. | Sell, change stock. |

Make sure **a manager is on every shift**: without one, nobody can approve a refund or witness a cash drop.

## The rules that keep your money safe

1. **Everyone has their own account and PIN.** Never share. Everything is recorded against the person signed in.
2. **Staff never approve their own work.** Not cash-ups, not breakages, not purchase orders. The system enforces this. You (the owner) and Tessera support may approve your own entries, because Tessera has switched that on for Epic Lounge.
3. **Sales are never edited or deleted.** A mistake is fixed with a return, so the history stays honest.
4. **Count before posting.** Deliveries and counts cannot be changed afterwards.
5. **M-PESA: see the message.** Cashiers type the code only after seeing the customer's confirmation message. You match the codes every evening.
6. **Lock the till when stepping away.** It also locks itself after 2 minutes.
7. **Cash drops during busy nights.** Don't let too much cash sit in the drawer.

## Warning signs to act on

| You see | It may mean | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The same cashier often short | Wrong change, or money taken | Watch their shifts; check their voids and discounts. |
| Many voids or removed items after a sale is rung up | Sales cancelled after the money was taken | Check the *Voids, discounts & price overrides* report and who approved. |
| An M-PESA code with no payment | A fake or reused code | Ask the cashier the same day; check the customer's message. |
| Stock count differences on the same drinks | Pouring too much, free drinks or theft | Count those shelves more often; check the open bottles (tots). |
| Many "sold below zero stock" approvals | Stock not received in the system | Make sure every delivery is received in *Receive stock*. |

## When something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A staff member forgot their password or PIN | Reset it yourself (see *How do I…*). |
| Till says "Outside licensed hours" | The licence hours lock. Check *Settings → Sales screen → Licensed hours*. |
| Till says a drink is out of stock | It wasn't received. A manager approves the sale, then receive or count the stock. |
| Nothing prints | Printer on, paper in, cable in → till menu → *Printer → Print test page*. Receipts can be reprinted. |
| A till device is lost or stolen | *Administration → Branches → Tills → Disconnect*, straight away. |
| The internet is down | The till keeps selling cash and card. For M-PESA, cashiers type the code. Don't switch off the till until the sales have been sent. |
| Anything else | Contact Tessera support with the screen name, what you tried, and a photo of the screen. |
